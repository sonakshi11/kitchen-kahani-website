Our Kahani

It started for one daughter. Now, it's for every kitchen.

This is Vibha's kitchen. And this is her kahani. Mom has been a nutritionist for over 20 years, and in all that time, she's never let a shortcut near our food. Everything was made at home, always. Four years ago, when her daughter moved abroad, she couldn't send her off without her ghar ka khaana. So she started making premixes and frozen snacks just for her, packed with the same care as if she'd never left.

What began as one mom looking after her daughter has slowly become something she wants to share, with anyone juggling a busy life who still wants to eat well, anyone done with preservatives, anyone far from home and craving something that tastes like it. She's a certified health coach now too, but honestly, she's been living this way for twenty years already. Zero shortcuts. Now, for your kitchen too.

Our kahani, unfiltered.

How To Make

From freezer to plate in minutes

No thawing, no fuss. Just three steps between your freezer and the first bite.

STEP 01

Bring to Room Temperature

Take out the quantity you need and let it sit for a few minutes before cooking.

STEP 02

Shallow or Deep Fry

Fry on medium heat until golden brown for the best texture and taste.

STEP 03

Serve Hot

Pair with your favourite chutney or dip and serve immediately.
